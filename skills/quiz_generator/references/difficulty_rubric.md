# Difficulty Rubric — quiz-generator skill

## Beginner (mastery < 40%)
- Tests direct recall of definitions and basic facts
- Single-step reasoning
- All 4 options clearly distinct
- Example: "What is the formula for the area of a rectangle?"

## Intermediate (mastery 40–74%)
- Requires applying a concept to a new situation
- Two-step reasoning
- Distractors target common misconceptions
- Example: "If a rectangle has area 48 and width 6, what is its length?"

## Advanced (mastery ≥ 75%)
- Requires synthesis or evaluation
- Multi-step reasoning or edge case
- Distractors require genuine understanding to eliminate
- Example: "Which statement about quadratic equations is FALSE and why?"

## Universal Quality Checks
- [ ] Exactly one unambiguously correct answer
- [ ] All distractors are plausible (would fool a student who partially understands)
- [ ] Question is clear and unambiguous
- [ ] Explanation teaches the concept, not just confirms the answer
- [ ] No "all of the above" or "none of the above" options
